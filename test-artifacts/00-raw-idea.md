# Raw Product Idea (Test Input)

## Initial Concept

"I want to build an app that helps remote workers stay focused during their work-from-home days. I've noticed that people (including myself) get distracted by household chores, social media, and random tasks when working from home. There should be a better way to block distractions and track focus time."

## What I'm Thinking

- Maybe something like a Pomodoro timer but smarter
- Could block distracting websites during focus sessions
- Track how much actual work time you get in
- Maybe add accountability - like tell someone if you're not staying focused?
- Should work on desktop since that's where people work

## Who This Is For

People who work from home and struggle with focus. Especially those without a dedicated office space who are surrounded by distractions.

## Questions I Have

- Would people actually use this?
- Is this different enough from existing Pomodoro apps?
- Should it be free or paid?
- Do I need both desktop and mobile?
